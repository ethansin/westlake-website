This Privacy Policy explains how Westlake Sleep Center ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit westlakesleep.com (the "Site") or receive text messages from us.

**Your health information.** How we use and disclose your protected health information is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices, as required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). If anything in this policy conflicts with that Notice regarding your health information, the Notice controls.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you call, fax, or visit our office, you may give us information such as your name, contact details, and insurance information. The Site does not currently have forms, accounts, or sign-ins.

**Your mobile phone number.** We receive your mobile phone number from you, or from your referring healthcare provider as part of your referral, so that we can schedule and coordinate your care.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit the Site, our hosting provider automatically processes basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, in order to deliver and secure the Site. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on the Site.

**Embedded and linked content.** Some pages include a YouTube video, shown in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and links to Google Maps. If you play the video or follow those links, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy.

## How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

- Schedule, confirm, and coordinate your appointments and care
- Send you text messages about your appointments (see below)
- Respond to your questions and requests
- Operate, maintain, and secure the Site
- Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations

## Text messages (SMS)

If you are scheduled for care with us, we may send text messages to the mobile number we have on file, such as appointment confirmations, reminders, preparation instructions, and other messages related to your care. We do not send marketing text messages.

- **Message frequency varies** depending on your appointments.
- **Message and data rates may apply**, according to your mobile plan.
- **To stop receiving texts**, reply **STOP** at any time. You will receive one message confirming that you've been unsubscribed.
- **For help**, reply **HELP** or call us at (650) 757-4813.

For full program details, see our [Terms & Conditions](/info/policies/terms).

## How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide our services, including with:

- **Service providers** that help us operate, such as our website host and our text messaging provider. They may use the information only to provide services to us, and where they handle health information, they are bound by HIPAA business associate agreements.
- **Your healthcare providers**, for treatment and care coordination, as permitted by law.
- **Authorities or others when required by law**, such as in response to a valid legal process.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

## How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Standard text messages are not encrypted and may appear on your phone's lock screen, so we keep the details in our texts limited. Please don't send sensitive health information to us by text message. Call us instead.

## How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, and as long as required by law, including medical record retention requirements.

## Your choices and rights

- You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying **STOP**.
- You can ask to receive communications from us in a different way, such as by phone.
- Your rights to access and amend your health records, and other rights regarding your health information, are described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law. To ask about them, contact us using the information below.

## Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Site.
